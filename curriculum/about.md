### About Project Mímir

Project Mímir is a free, open-source curriculum for people who want a career building AI systems, especially **agents**: software that uses language models and tools to complete multi-step work on its own.

It follows the method that made [The Odin Project](https://www.theodinproject.com/) work for web developers:

- **One path, in order.** You don't have to work out what to learn next.
- **Written lessons plus the best free resources.** Each lesson explains the idea, then points you to the best free material on the internet to go deeper.
- **Projects, constantly.** Every few lessons you build something real and put it on GitHub. By the end, your portfolio shows what you can do.

### Who it's for

Motivated beginners. You don't need any coding experience. If you already code, skim Foundations and use its projects as a test. If you can build them comfortably, move on.

The roles this prepares you for:

- **AI Engineer:** builds LLM features into products, such as retrieval, structured extraction, evals and APIs.
- **Agent / Automation Engineer:** designs tools and agent loops that get real work done.
- **Forward-Deployed / AI Solutions Engineer:** works with customers to turn their workflows into working AI systems.
- **LLMOps / Platform Engineer:** keeps AI systems observable, affordable, secure and reliable.

Heading to a master's degree or a research role? The elective Research Track covers the math, reinforcement learning, planning, multi-agent theory and research skills a degree expects.

### The paths

| Path | What you'll be able to do | Hours |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations | Use the terminal, Git, Python and data tools, work with AI coding assistants, and call your first language model | ~260 |
| AI Engineering Core | Build reliable LLM apps: prompting, evals, structured outputs, classic ML, backends, RAG and a TypeScript frontend | ~380 |
| Agentic Systems | Build, connect, secure and evaluate agents | ~300 |
| Production & LLMOps | Deploy, observe and scale what you build | ~180 |
| ML Foundations (elective) | Understand how models work, serve open models and fine-tune them | ~120 |
| Research Track (elective, requires ML Foundations) | Learn the theory a master's expects: math, RL, planning, multi-agent systems, research skills | ~500 |
| Final Capstone | Solve a real problem for real users | ~120 |
| Getting Hired | Turn your portfolio into a job | ~100 |

The core paths take about 1,340 hours. At 15–20 hours a week that's roughly 16–21 months. ML Foundations adds about 120 hours, and the Research Track about 500. Treat the hours as guides, not deadlines.

### How to succeed

- **Go in order.** Each lesson assumes the ones before it.
- **Do every project.** Reading without building is how people get stuck in tutorial hell. If a project feels too hard, it's probably the right size.
- **Build in public.** Push every project to GitHub, and write about anything you found hard.
- **Use AI to learn, not to skip learning.** Ask models to explain errors and review your code. Don't let them write your projects until you could write them yourself.
- **Expect to spend a few dollars.** Most projects cost a few dollars in API calls. Free tiers and local models cover nearly everything.
